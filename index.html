About this experiment

For a homogeneous solid of height H whose horizontal sectional area is A(z) = Czp, the centroid is the mean height and the volume-bisecting plane lies at the median height. The case p = 1 includes a truncated paraboloid; p = 2 includes a cone. For p > 2, the area profile is mathematically valid but cannot come from a three-dimensional convex solid.

This laboratory is a companion to the expository manuscript Bisecting Is Not Balancing: How Far Apart?. Source code, mathematical notes, and citation information are maintained in the MeanMedianLab repository.
